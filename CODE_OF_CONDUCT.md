# Community conduct

Be patient, specific and respectful. Discuss code and evidence rather than
attacking a person. Harassment, discrimination and disclosing another person's
private information are not welcome.

Maintainer: [Jovi](https://github.com/L-Jovi). Report ordinary moderation concerns
through an issue without repeating private or abusive material. The maintainer
may remove harmful content and restrict repeated disruptive participation.
