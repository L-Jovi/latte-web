A generator compiled by BabelBabel 编译出的 generator

This page runs what Babel made from the genn generator in index.js, with the regenerator runtime it needs. The guide shows each value sent back into yield and lets you call next() yourself. Then read transform-generator.js: the generator has become a switch statement that remembers where it stopped.

这个页面运行 Babel 为 index.js 里的 generator genn 生成的代码，并加载了它需要的 regenerator 运行时。说明面板会展示每个送回 yield 的值，也让你亲手调用 next()。然后读一读 transform-generator.js：generator 变成了一条 switch 语句，记得自己停在了哪里。
